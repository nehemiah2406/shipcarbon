Key findings

34%

Container ships

14% of the fleet but a third of the CO2 in 2025 (51.7 Mt from 2,430 ships).

48%

One ship in ten

The top 10% of ships emitted almost half the CO2 in 2025, up from 42% in 2018.

−12%

COVID dip

CO2 fell from 147 to 130 Mt in 2020 and only passed the 2019 level again in 2024.

+4.2 Mt

The 2025 rise

Comes from newly covered ships (+6.6 Mt). Ships reported in both 2024 and 2025 emitted 6.3% less.

~60%

LNG carriers and methane

2% of ships, but 59% of all reported methane in 2025 (50,500 t CH4).
